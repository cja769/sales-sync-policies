# Disconnect Sales Sync

1. Sign in to the QuickBooks Online company you want to disconnect as an authorized administrator.
2. Go to **Integrations** and select **Manage integrations**.
3. Find **Sales Sync**, open its menu, and select **Disconnect**. Follow QuickBooks’ prompts to confirm.
4. Email [aisenbreyjay@gmail.com](mailto:aisenbreyjay@gmail.com?subject=Sales%20Sync%20disconnect) with the business name so the operator can remove the active connection settings and stop any scheduled runs for that company.

Previously posted entries remain in QuickBooks. See [Intuit’s instructions for managing integrations](https://quickbooks.intuit.com/learn-support/en-us/help-article/mobile-apps/fix-connected-app-issues-quickbooks-online/L7At9OGp4_US_en_US).
