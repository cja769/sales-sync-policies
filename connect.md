# Connect or reconnect Sales Sync

Sales Sync connections are managed by the operator. An authorized QuickBooks administrator can email [aisenbreyjay@gmail.com](mailto:aisenbreyjay@gmail.com?subject=Sales%20Sync%20connection) to arrange authorization or restore a connection. Include the business name and whether this is a first connection or a reconnection.

When the operator starts authorization, sign in on Intuit’s consent page and select the correct QuickBooks company. Third Coast Homebrew uses Lightspeed. Ultimate Pet Spa uses MoeGo. The operator verifies the company and runs a journal preview before enabling live posting.

Keep passwords, OAuth codes, and tokens out of email.
